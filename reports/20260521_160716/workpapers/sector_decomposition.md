## Country-Level Notes

### 🇮🇪 Ireland (+12.3% GDP)
Dominated by **B-E Industry excl. construction (+29.1% GVA, +6.55 pp)**, almost entirely driven by **manufacturing (+31.0%)** in pharma, chemicals, and medical devices — sectors where multinational IP transfers and export front-loading ahead of US tariff policy are key distortions. **J Information & communication (+11.5%)** adds a further +1.53 pp. Offsets come from **M-N Professional/scientific/technical (-4.0%, -0.78 pp)** and **O-Q Public admin/health (-0.32 pp).** Ireland's GDP remains heavily distorted by MNC activity; modified GNI (GNI*) would show a far lower underlying growth rate. [[1]]

### 🇩🇪 Germany (+0.1% GDP)
Near-stagnation. **F Construction (-2.9%, -0.13 pp)** and **K Financial & insurance (-3.6%, -0.13 pp)** are the biggest drags. **G-I Trade/transport/accommodation (+1.3%, +0.19 pp)** and **O-Q Public admin/health (+1.5%, +0.27 pp)** provide modest offsets. Manufacturing (C) contracted -1.0%, reflecting ongoing automotive sector headwinds and weak external demand. [[2]]

### 🇫🇮 Finland (+0.2% GDP)
Balanced but fragile recovery. **J ICT (+5.1%, +0.28 pp)** and **B-E Industry (+1.5%, +0.28 pp)** are equally the top positive contributors. Key drags: **K Financial & insurance (-4.6%, -0.16 pp)** and **G-I Trade/transport/accommodation (-1.4%, -0.18 pp)**. **R-U Arts/other services (-3.3%, -0.09 pp)** also a notable drag. Agriculture outperformed (+3.8%) but has low weight. [[3]]

### 🇪🇪 Estonia (+0.3% GDP)
Sharp internal divergence. **G-I Trade/transport/accommodation collapsed (-7.5%, -1.20 pp)** — the single largest sectoral drag in absolute pp among all 10 countries relative to country GDP. **B-E Industry (+5.0%, +0.74 pp)** and **J ICT (+7.0%, +0.57 pp)** rescued overall growth. **A Agriculture (-10.2%, -0.18 pp)** also a headwind. Real estate (+4.2%, +0.38 pp) provided a minor positive. [[4]]

### 🇮🇹 Italy (+0.4% GDP)
Broad-based but very weak growth. **G-I Trade/transport/accommodation (+1.0%, +0.19 pp)** is the largest positive contributor. **F Construction (+2.4%, +0.12 pp)** continued to benefit from Superbonus-related renovation activity winding down gradually. **O-Q Public admin/health (-0.9%, -0.12 pp)** is the primary drag. Manufacturing sub-sector contracted (-0.3%), suggesting ongoing industrial weakness. [[5]]

### 🇲🇹 Malta (+3.8% GDP)
Strong services-led growth. **G-I Trade/transport/accommodation (+8.8%, +1.49 pp)** is by far the dominant engine — reflecting tourism and hospitality recovery. **O-Q Public admin/education/health (+5.6%, +0.78 pp)** and **J ICT (+6.9%, +0.81 pp)** are co-contributors. **A Agriculture collapsed (-23.2%, -0.11 pp)** but this sector has minimal weight in Malta's economy. Industry essentially flat (-0.1%).

### 🇭🇺 Hungary (+0.5% GDP)
Held down by **B-E Industry excl. construction (-2.5%, -0.47 pp)**, with manufacturing (-2.6%) reflecting sustained weakness in automotive/EV transition. **G-I Trade/transport (+2.0%, +0.30 pp)**, **O-Q Public admin/health (+1.5%, +0.22 pp)**, and **M-N Professional/technical (+2.4%, +0.21 pp)** provide the offsetting growth. **A Agriculture (-4.3%, -0.12 pp)** also a drag.

### 🇨🇾 Cyprus (+3.8% GDP)
Broad-based and robust. All sectors grew or were flat — no sector contributed negatively. **G-I Trade/transport/accommodation (+6.4%, +1.31 pp)** leads, consistent with Cyprus's tourism-driven economy. **J ICT (+8.0%, +0.83 pp)** reflects continued growth of the island's financial/tech services hub. **F Construction (+4.5%, +0.22 pp)** also healthy.

### 🇦🇹 Austria (+0.4% GDP)
Recovery skewed toward domestic services. **O-Q Public admin/education/health (+2.8%, +0.49 pp)** is the main engine. **L Real estate (+2.7%, +0.24 pp)** contributes positively. Key drags: **F Construction (-2.9%, -0.17 pp)** — mirroring Germany — and **B-E Industry (-0.7%, -0.13 pp)**, with manufacturing (+1.2%) a positive outlier within Industry. **M-N Professional/scientific (-1.2%, -0.11 pp)** also weighs.

### 🇱🇺 Luxembourg (+0.6% GDP)
Financial-centre dynamics. **O-Q Public admin/education/health (+4.0%, +0.68 pp)** is the top contributor, unusual for Luxembourg but reflecting public investment. **K Financial & insurance (+1.1%, +0.28 pp)** provides support. Main drag: **B-E Industry excl. construction (-6.7%, -0.35 pp)**, with manufacturing (-3.2%) contracting sharply. **M-N Professional/technical (-1.7%, -0.22 pp)** also negative.
